# Management Dashboard — Product Analytics Overview

## One-page executive layout

### KPI cards
- Total Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Profit Margin
- Units Sold
- Revenue YoY %

### Visual 1 — Revenue by Country
Horizontal bar chart, descending revenue. Add revenue share tooltip and drill-through to country/year detail.

### Visual 2 — Revenue by Date and Year
Line chart by month/date with year as legend. Include prior-year comparison where the dataset supports a valid date dimension.

### Visual 3 — Profit and Unit Sales YoY Change
Combo or two aligned KPI visuals showing current year, prior year and percentage change. Avoid dual-axis distortion where possible.

### Visual 4 — Revenue by Discount Band
Column/donut visual showing revenue and revenue share by discount band. Include gross margin as tooltip to prevent revenue-only decisions.

### Visual 5 — Detailed Table
Country | Year | Revenue | Total Cost | Gross Profit | Margin | Units Sold | YoY Revenue %

## Executive filters
- Year
- Country
- Category
- Brand
- Discount Band

## UX rules
- Use consistent currency and percentage formatting.
- Default to latest available period.
- Make negative YoY changes visually obvious.
- Keep the page readable at 16:9.
- Add a last-refresh timestamp.

## Management decision questions
1. Which countries should receive additional commercial attention?
2. Is revenue growth translating into profit growth?
3. Are high-discount bands creating volume but destroying margin?
4. Which products/categories drive disproportionate profit?
5. Which countries or periods require investigation?
