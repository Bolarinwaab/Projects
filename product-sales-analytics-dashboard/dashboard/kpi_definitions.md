# KPI Definitions

| KPI | Definition | Management use |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Sale Price × Units Sold | Measures sales value |
| Total Cost | Cost Price × Units Sold | Measures direct product cost |
| Gross Profit | Revenue − Total Cost | Measures contribution before other operating costs |
| Profit Margin | Gross Profit ÷ Revenue | Measures profitability of revenue |
| Units Sold | Sum of Units Sold | Measures sales volume |
| Average Selling Price | Revenue ÷ Units Sold | Tracks realised selling price |
| Revenue YoY % | (Current Revenue − Prior Revenue) ÷ Prior Revenue | Measures annual growth |
| Profit YoY % | (Current Profit − Prior Profit) ÷ Prior Profit | Tests whether growth is profitable |
| Units YoY % | (Current Units − Prior Units) ÷ Prior Units | Measures volume growth |
