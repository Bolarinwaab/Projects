# Management Insights Framework

## What management should receive

The dashboard should not stop at charts. Each reporting cycle should produce five concise conclusions:

1. **Growth:** identify whether revenue, units and gross profit increased or declined year over year.
2. **Geography:** identify the leading and lagging countries by revenue and profit, not revenue alone.
3. **Profitability:** compare revenue growth against profit growth and margin movement.
4. **Discount effectiveness:** compare revenue share and margin across discount bands.
5. **Product concentration:** identify products/categories contributing the greatest revenue and gross profit.

## Decision rules

- Revenue up + profit up: generally healthy growth; investigate sustainability.
- Revenue up + profit down: investigate discounting, product mix and cost pressure.
- Units up + revenue flat/down: investigate price/mix deterioration.
- High discount + low margin: review promotional economics.
- High revenue + low margin: protect volume while investigating pricing/cost structure.
- Low revenue + high margin: assess whether targeted commercial investment can scale demand.

## Data-quality gate

No management conclusion should be presented until key relationships, dates, country values, units and pricing fields have passed validation.
